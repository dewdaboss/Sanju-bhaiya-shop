# Google Business Profile — Sanju bhaiya ke liye checklist

Aapne Google Business Profile banana choose kiya hai. Yeh website se alag Google account/listing process hai. Exact address aur aapke Google account ki access/verification ke bina hum listing create ya verify nahi kar sakte. Neeche copy-ready details hain; jo information missing hai usse confirm karke hi submit karein.

## Copy-ready details

- **Business name:** Iconic Car Washing Centre
- **Primary category suggestion:** Car wash (Google mein available exact category choose karein)
- **Phone:** +91 79700 00601
- **Description draft:** Iconic Car Washing Centre offers car washing, vacuum cleaning, dashboard and tyre polish, body polish, a complete care package, and VIP wash and polish. Contact Sanju bhaiya for service availability, timings and bookings.
- **Photos:** `public/images/iconic-signboard.jpg` and `public/images/shop-rate-board.jpg` are your supplied shop photos. Use real exterior/interior/team photos for the listing.
- **Do not upload the generated hero as a real shop photo.** It is illustrative, not a photograph of the actual premises.
- **Hours:** Abhi confirm nahi. Actual opening/closing time aur weekly off enter karein.
- **Address / service area:** Abhi confirm nahi. Customers ko sirf actual visitable shop address dena chahiye.
- **Website:** Public hosting ke baad actual HTTPS website URL add karein; sandbox preview URL permanent listing ke liye use na karein.
- **Booking link:** Hosted website ka `/#book` URL, after live hours/durations are configured and tested.

## Setup steps

1. Sanju bhaiya ke control wale Google account se Google Business Profile kholen: https://business.google.com/
2. Business name enter karein. Pehle search karein ki existing listing to nahi hai. Existing profile ho to duplicate banane ke bajay access/ownership manage karein.
3. Suitable business category choose karein.
4. Actual physical shop location/address aur service-area details enter karein. Map pin ko premises par verify karein.
5. Confirmed phone number aur hosted website add karein.
6. Actual hours set karein. Holiday hours bhi update karte rahein.
7. Google ke available methods se ownership verify karein (video, phone, email ya doosra offered method). Verification complete hone tak listing fully managed/public nahi hogi.
8. Real shop photos, confirmed services aur description add karein. Website ka booking link tabhi add karein jab live schedule test ho chuka ho.
9. Listing mein fake reviews na likhein. Real customers se honest feedback maang sakte hain.

Google ke exact screens/methods change ho sakte hain; apne account mein dikhne wale official instructions follow karein. Is task mein koi listing submit/verify nahi ki gayi.
